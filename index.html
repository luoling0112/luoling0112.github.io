Biography

My research interests focus on GeoAI, Computer Vision, and Spatiotemporal Data Analysis. I explore how computer vision and geospatial technologies can help address urban sustainability challenges, including smart city development, urban environmental monitoring, and sustainable urban planning.

My current work explores interpretable spatial learning, multimodal geospatial understanding, and data-driven urban analysis. I am actively seeking research opportunities and collaborations related to geospatial artificial intelligence, urban computing, and spatially informed machine learning.

Feel free to contact me by email to discuss research opportunities or academic collaboration.
